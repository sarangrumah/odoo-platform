# Gentle Woman — Headless Commerce · Executive One-Pager

**For:** Board of Directors  **Owner:** Ade Maryadi (Erajaya Active Lifestyle)  **Status:** Live pilot · tenant `gentlewoman`

---

## The need
Gentle Woman needs a **brand-defining online store** that runs on **real, governed systems** — not a generic template that risks stock, price and order mismatches. Off-the-shelf webshops force a choice between brand experience and operational control.

## The solution
A **headless storefront**: a fast, fully branded Next.js website with **Odoo as the single source of truth** for catalog, stock, price, orders, customers, promotions and fulfilment. Editorial content (banners, campaigns, announcements) is managed **inside Odoo** — **no second system to license or operate.**

## Delivered today (live pilot)
- **Shopping:** product browse/detail, size availability, promo pricing, "New" drops, search & filters — bilingual (ID/EN).
- **Omnichannel:** store locator, **real-time in-store stock**, **click-&-collect**.
- **Customers:** accounts + **guest checkout**, saved shipping/billing addresses, order history, wishlist.
- **Growth:** **affiliate program** (trackable links, commission, payouts, self-serve dashboard), promotions, newsletter, editorial CMS in Odoo.
- **Trust:** HTTPS everywhere, **encrypted sessions & customer data at rest**, hardened against common web attacks, **UU PDP** consent.

## Business value
| Lever | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Brand-led experience | Higher conversion; the storefront is a brand asset, not a rented page |
| One source of truth | No stock/price/order reconciliation; fewer errors, lower support cost |
| Omnichannel | In-store stock + pickup capture demand into physical stores |
| Low TCO | Content runs inside Odoo — no separate CMS license/server/team |
| Affiliate engine | Measurable, commission-based acquisition |
| Compliance & security | Lower regulatory and reputational risk |

## Status & the one blocker
Phase 1 is **built, integrated and verified end-to-end.** The single dependency to full go-live is the **Eraspace payment integration** — checkout is complete through order creation and activates the moment the vendor's API spec arrives. An optional **AI personal shopper** is a budget-gated Phase-3 differentiator, off the launch critical path.
