The 12 core functions of a WMS

📥

1 · Receiving / Inbound

Book goods against ASN/PO, capture lot, expiry, qty; reconcile over/short.

📦

2 · Putaway

Direct each pallet to the right bin by rules — not "anywhere there's space".

🗺️

3 · Slotting / Storage

Bin layout, capacity, zones; place fast movers where they cut travel.

🛒

4 · Picking

Single, batch, cluster, wave — convert orders into an efficient pick path.

📤

5 · Packing & Shipping

Pack into cartons, weigh, label, manifest to the carrier, hand over.

🔁

6 · Replenishment

Keep pick faces full from reserve; min/max & demand-driven transfers.

🔢

7 · Cycle Counting

Continuous counting to hold inventory accuracy without a full stop-count.

🧬

8 · Traceability

Lot / serial / expiry, FEFO, full genealogy for recall & compliance.

📡

9 · Barcode / RF Mobility

Scan-driven work on handhelds; offline-tolerant; no paper.

✅

10 · Quality / QC

Inbound & outbound inspection gates, holds, NCR & corrective action.

🏷️

11 · Labeling & Print

Product, location, pallet (SSCC) & shipping labels to thermal printers.

📊

12 · Reporting / KPI / Audit

Accuracy, throughput, dock-to-stock, who-did-what immutable trail.

Part B · Deep Dive 1 — Inbound

Receiving & directed putaway

What WMS requires

  • Book against ASN/PO with lot, expiry, serial capture at the dock.
  • Inspection gate before stock becomes available.
  • Directed putaway — system tells the operator the best bin, by tiered rules (fixed → fast-mover zone → nearest empty → volume fit → temperature).
  • High throughput — receipts of thousands of lines must not stall.

What's built here

  • custom_wms_putaway — a custom.putaway.engine that scores every candidate bin 0–100 across 7 strategies and proposes a destination.
  • Confidence auto-apply: score > 90 rewrites the move-line destination automatically; lower scores surface to the handheld for operator review.
  • Strategy presets: zwme001_6tier, abc, fefo, custom — one active strategy per warehouse, enforced.
  • custom_receipt_async validates huge receipts in the background via queue_job (no browser timeout).
  • Incoming QC via custom_quality_full + scan-in via custom_barcode / HHT Receive page.

Part B · Deep Dive 1b — Putaway Engine

Seven pluggable putaway strategies, tier-prioritised

SAP-style ZWME001 logic in owned code: rules are grouped into tiers; the lowest tier with a positive score wins, ties broken by score. Generic — no vertical assumptions.

① fixed_location

Pre-assigned home bin → confidence 100.

② nearest_empty

First bin with zero quants → 85.

③ zone_round_robin

Even spread across a zone → 70.

④ by_volume

Best volumetric fit vs bin capacity → 60–100; rejects oversize.

⑤ by_temperature

Matches ambient / chilled / frozen zone → 75.

⑥ by_abc_velocity

Fast movers near dock — A=95, B=75, C=55.

⑦ custom_python

Operator-defined expression evaluated in a sandbox (safe_eval), returning (location_id, score) — for site-specific logic without a code change. Dangerous builtins are blocked.

Part B · Deep Dive 2 — Internal & Outbound

Picking & rule-driven replenishment

What WMS requires

  • Efficient pick paths — group orders so the picker walks once, not once per order.
  • Batch & cluster picking — scan once, distribute across many orders / cluster by source bin.
  • Keep pick faces full — replenish from reserve before they run dry.
  • Move stock proactively — consolidate half-bins, pull expiring lots, pre-stage for today's shipments.

What's built here

  • custom_barcode — batch session (auto-distributes scanned qty across pickings) and cluster run (groups lines by source location for one efficient walk).
  • custom_wms_to_engine — a transfer-order engine firing on 5 triggers:
low-water markexpiry approaching → scrapzone consolidationpicking replenishment (24h horizon)manual
  • Proposals materialize into real stock.move transfers, with a barcoded pick-slip QWeb report and a daily cron.

Part B · Deep Dive 3 — Accuracy

Cycle counting & lot/expiry traceability

Cycle counting — perpetual accuracy

  • Plan-driven sampling: ABC-velocity, random, by-zone, by-value, last-counted.
  • Session → line model with assigned counters; variance qty, %, and value rolled up automatically.
  • Approval gate: only a supervisor can post a variance adjustment (creates the corrective stock.move).
  • Daily cron generates new sessions from due plans — no manual scheduling.

Traceability & FEFO

  • Native CE: full lot/serial tracking, expiration dates, FEFO removal, upstream/downstream traceability.
  • FEFO slotting as a putaway strategy preset.
  • Expiry-approaching trigger auto-routes near-expiry lots to a scrap/quarantine location within a configurable window.
  • GS1 capture: handhelds parse GTIN, lot, expiry, serial, weight from a single barcode (Application Identifiers).

Part B · Trust

Security, audit & multi-tenancy

🧾

Audit on every move

WMS records mix in the pdp.audited.mixin and mail.thread — who changed what, when, with chatter. HHT scans are append-only and immutable.

🛡️

Hardened device edge

HMAC-signed requests, nonce replay protection, timestamp drift checks and per-device CIDR allow-lists keep rogue scanners out.

🏢

True multi-tenant

DB-per-tenant isolation; devices, audit logs and stock are scoped per tenant — one platform, many independent warehouses.

Approval gates are built in where they matter: putaway auto-apply has a confidence threshold, and cycle-count variance posting is supervisor-only. Nothing silently rewrites stock.

Part C · Recommendation

A pragmatic path to close the gaps

Now — high value, low effort

• WMS KPI dashboard (accuracy, dock-to-stock, picks/hr) from existing scan logs.
• Wave-lite: release batches by ship-date from the TO engine.
• Carrier connector for the primary 3PL via the adapter framework.

Next — differentiators

• Dock-door / appointment scheduling module.
• Cartonization & pack-bench flow.
• Re-slotting recommendations from ABC velocity history.

Later — only if demanded

• Labor-management standards & gamified KPIs.
• Voice / pick-to-light integration.
• EDI/ASN automation for enterprise trading partners.

Build vs integrate: for the “Later” column, integrating a specialist WMS at the edge is often cheaper than rebuilding it — the platform already exposes the secure API and audit surface to do so cleanly.

Appendix A · Data Model

Custom WMS objects & key fields

All models mix in pdp.audited.mixin + mail.thread and extend native stock.* — no forks of Odoo core.

custom_wms_putaway

  • custom.wms.putaway.strategy — rule_set (ZWME001/ABC/FEFO), warehouse_id, auto_apply
  • custom.wms.putaway.rule — tier 1–6, kind ×7, target/domain, abc_class, temperature_zone, custom_python
  • custom.wms.putaway.suggestion — move_line_id, score, status: pending→applied/overridden/rejected
  • custom.putaway.engine — propose() · apply_top_proposal() (>90 auto)

custom_wms_cycle_count

  • custom.cycle.count.plan — method (ABC/random/zone/value/last), frequency, next_run_date
  • custom.cycle.count.session — state: draft→in_progress→reviewing→closed; variance_count/value
  • custom.cycle.count.line — expected/counted/variance_qty, status
  • custom.cycle.count.adjustment — stock_move_id, approved_by_id

custom_wms_to_engine

  • custom.to.rule — trigger ×5, low_water_qty, expiry_days_ahead, source/target domains
  • custom.transfer.order — source/target_location_id, planned/actual_qty, state, stock_move_id
  • custom.to.engine — evaluate_all() · materialize() → stock.move

custom_hht_bridge

  • hht.device — device_id, model (Zebra/Honeywell/browser), api_key/secret, allowed_cidrs, status
  • hht.scan.log — action ×6, barcode, location_id, qty, result, client_ip (append-only)
  • hht.sync.queue — client_id, payload, state: queued→applied/deduped

custom_barcode

  • custom.barcode.scan.session / batch.session / cluster.run — picking(s), state, lines
  • custom.barcode.scan.line — product_id, lot_id, raw_barcode, GS1 (gtin/lot/exp/serial)
  • custom.label.template · printer.config (Zebra/ESC-POS/CUPS) · print.queue

custom_quality_full

  • quality.point — operation (in/mfg/out), check_kind ×4, measure_min/max
  • quality.check — state (waiting/pass/fail), measure_value, alert_id, inspection lines
  • quality.alert (NCR) · custom.quality.capa (action_type) · quality.signature

Integration, crons & audit

  • Native base — stock.location / lot / quant / move(.line) / picking / warehouse; labels via QWeb-PDF.
